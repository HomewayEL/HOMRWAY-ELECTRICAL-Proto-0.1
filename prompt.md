# Website Development Prompt — Homeway Electrical

Create a **modern, responsive, professional website for Homeway Electrical** based on the existing prototype design in `ProtoHomewayElectrical.zip`.

The prototype uses a **dark navy background, gold/yellow accent colors, electric-blue glow effects, centered Homeway Electrical logo, modern typography, subtle grid patterns, and electrical-themed visual effects**. Preserve this overall visual identity, but expand the website into a complete multi-section business and team portfolio website.

## Main Website Objective

The website should clearly present the services offered by Homeway Electrical and introduce the people behind the company. The home page should directly display important service information and provide clear calls to action:

- **Book a Home Visit**
- **Request Electrical Service**
- **Schedule an Inspection**
- **Contact an Electrician**
- **View Our Team**

## Design Direction

Use a modern and professional style suitable for a residential electrical service company.

### Visual Style

- Dark navy or deep blue background
- Gold/yellow primary accent color
- Electric-blue glow effects
- White and light-gray text
- Rounded cards with soft shadows
- Subtle animated grid or circuit-board background
- Electrical icons for each service
- Clean, bold, modern typography
- Smooth hover animations
- Fully responsive layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Use the existing Homeway Electrical logo from the prototype
- Maintain the original brand identity and color palette
- Make the site look reliable, safe, modern, and professional

Avoid making the website look overly futuristic, crowded, or difficult to read. The design should feel trustworthy and easy to navigate.

## Navigation Menu

Create a sticky responsive navigation bar with these menu items:

1. Home
2. Services
3. How It Works
4. Our Team
5. About
6. Contact

On mobile devices, convert the navigation into a hamburger menu with a smooth open and close animation. Keep a highlighted **Book a Home Visit** button in the navigation.
